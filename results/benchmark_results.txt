Complexity Summary
----------------------------------------
insertion_sort : best O(n), worst O(n²)
binary_search  : best O(1), worst O(log n)  [requires sorted input]
linear_search  : best O(1), worst O(n)

Analysis (sort-first trade-off)
----------------------------------------
At n=3000, insertion_sort costs ~4.5 M comparisons (worst case O(n²)).
Once the list is sorted, every binary search costs at most ~24 comparisons (O(log n)).
Linear search on the unsorted list costs up to 3000 comparisons per query.
For a team that sorts/views tasks many times a day but adds tasks infrequently,
the one-time sort cost is amortised over many binary searches — each of which is
~125× cheaper than a linear scan at n=3000.  The sort-first strategy is therefore
worth it for TaskFlow's read-heavy usage pattern.
